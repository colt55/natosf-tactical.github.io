SENTINEL // Master deployment architecture

One campaign framework. Different battlefields.

SENTINEL separates a global operator career from deployment-specific battlefield state. A new deployment can change terrain, base placement, Areas of Operation and objective geometry without resetting the player career or rebuilding the core campaign systems.

Global persistence

One Operator Career

Military rank, career points and specialization XP continue across SENTINEL deployments. The active Operator Role is assigned by the multiplayer lobby slot.

Deployment persistence

Independent Battlefields

AO progress, QRF state, vehicles, aircraft, objectives, tickets, logistics and battlefield positions remain isolated to each deployment.

Enemy response

QRF Wave Director

Player-scaled hidden reinforcement waves dispatch one maximum-skill ground or air platform at unpredictable combat-progress intervals.

Objectives

Two Terrain Object Families

Deployments use the approved Arma 3 + official DLC objective library or the CUP Terrain Core objective library.

Command

CENTCOM Tablet

Mission information, OPERATOR ROLE status, rank progression, logistics, Blue Force tracking and Officer tools provide a common command interface on every deployment.

Sustainment

Logistics & Recovery

Persistent fleet systems, towing, recovery, tactical lift and field sustainment remain standardized across deployments.

SENTINEL // Global career

The battlefield changes. Your operator career does not.

SENTINEL maintains a Steam UID-based operator record. Rank points and specialization XP remain global while each deployment maintains its own operational state. The multiplayer lobby slot selects the active Operator Role for the session.

Career rank

29 Military Ranks

Persistent career points drive promotion from Private through General of the Army.

Role development

Independent Specialization XP

Every Operator Role has its own persistent XP record. Changing lobby slots changes the active role without erasing progression.

Equipment

Ranked Arsenal

Lobby-assigned role and XP tier determine authorized controlled equipment, including equipment carried inside inventory containers.

Continuity

Deployment-Proof Progression

Officer battlefield resets and terrain changes do not erase the Global Career database.
