Built for continuity, not one-off missions.

The campaign carries meaningful state between sessions. Player development, operational activity, Blue Force assets and recovery systems are designed around long-term cooperative play.

Persistent Campaign

SENTINEL // Signature progression system

The battlefield changes. Your operator career does not.

SENTINEL maintains a persistent military career for every operator. Rank, career PTS, combat record, selected specialization, specialization XP and Ranked Arsenal progression continue across mission updates and new operational deployments—including changes of terrain, faction, playable slot or character.

Career rank

29 military ranks

Earn career PTS through combat and mission success. Promotion is calculated from the persistent operator record, from Private through General of the Army.

Role development

Independent specialization XP

RECON, DEMOLITIONS, SNIPER, ANTI-ARMOR, HEAVY GUNNER and MEDIC each maintain their own persistent XP progression.

Equipment authorization

Ranked Arsenal

Your selected specialization and its XP tier determine access to role-specific equipment. Higher tiers are cumulative, so previously unlocked equipment remains authorized.

Campaign continuity

One operator career

A new map, faction or deployment is a new battlefield—not a new career. The operator record carries forward as SENTINEL evolves.
