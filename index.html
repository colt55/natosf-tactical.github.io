SENTINEL // CAMPAIGN INFORMATION

Operation Sentinel

Operation Sentinel is the persistent cooperative tactical campaign of the NATO Special Forces Tactical Unit on Livonia.

New and experienced Arma 3 players are welcome. Sentinel is built around serious tactical gameplay, teamwork and persistent consequences without unnecessary bureaucracy.

Player actions matter between sessions. Campaign state, player progression, equipment, vehicles and operational conditions persist across server restarts.

Operations span infantry combat, reconnaissance, medical support, anti-armor warfare, demolitions, heavy weapons, aviation, logistics, recovery and combined-arms missions across hostile-controlled areas of Livonia.

SENTINEL // COMBAT SYSTEMS

ACE, ACM & ACRE2

Operation Sentinel uses ACE3 and Advanced Combat Medicine to provide detailed tactical and casualty-management gameplay.

Medical gameplay includes bleeding, airway management, circulation, fractures, medication, transfusion, cardiac emergencies and casualty evacuation.

Tactical radio communications use ACRE2 through TeamSpeak.
