# NSFTU — Operation SENTINEL

Official information for **SENTINEL**, the persistent Arma 3 cooperative MilSim campaign of the **NATO Special Forces Tactical Unit (NSFTU)**.

## Campaign standard

- **Mission:** SENTINEL
- **Latest documented mission:** V2.04.472 **TEST BUILD** (not a claim of live-server deployment)
- **Active map / deployment:** **G.O.S Al Rayak** (`pja310`)
- **Mission capacity:** **20 players maximum**
- **BLUEFOR:** CUP Development US Army OCP equipment and vehicles, with mission-specific air, naval and logistics assets
- **OPFOR:** Russian Spetsnaz infantry and CUP Russian ground/air assets, plus Takistani militia
- **Objectives:** CUP terrain-object objectives with supported Arma 3 / official DLC object classes; dynamic AO selection, defenders, and persistent objective state
- **Gameplay:** Hardcore tactical co-op / MilSim, combined arms, logistics, aviation and role-specific Ranked Arsenal

SENTINEL's campaign and operator progression persist across restarts. Destroyed/completed objective states and battlefield assets are handled by deployment-specific persistence; historic career records are preserved across mission updates.

## Campaign capabilities

- Persistent, dynamic Areas of Operation with CUP/vanilla/DLC terrain objectives, guarded facilities and remote mechanized defenders where configured.
- Enemy patrols, virtual occupation and player-scaled QRF, with optional enemy-only headless client offloading.
- CENTCOM Tablet: battlefield map, role experience, logistics, Blue Force tracking and support.
- TALCS helicopter support, tactical airlift, recovery, towing and maritime operations.
- Ranked ACE Arsenal shared by the main base and **OVERLORD** command vehicle.
- Equipment access restricted by the **lobby-assigned role** and its earned tiers; role-specific equipment enforcement includes anti-looting checks.

## Blue Force capability

- **HUNTER 1–6:** six CUP M1240A1 tactical vehicles.
- **OVERLORD:** command and support vehicle.
- **RHIB 1–4:** rigid-hulled inflatable boats, supported by SDV and LCU maritime assets.
- **Aviation:** tactical lift / MEDEVAC aircraft and TALCS support.
- **Logistics:** transport, resupply, towing, recovery and role equipment crates.

The Tablet uses stable numbered fleet/crate labels for easier identification.

## Current playable operator roles

**RECON JTAC / Recon** · **Combat Specialist** · **Mortar** · **Sniper** · **AT/AD** · **Heavy Gunner** · **Medic** · **Helicopter Pilot**

The lobby slot determines your active role. There is **no playable Battle Captain**. Historic Battle Captain career records are retained only to protect saved data.

- Recon: **3 Arsenal tiers**, with the authorized UAV terminal and Recon-only underbarrel grenade-launcher rifles.
- Combat Specialist: **4 tiers**.
- Mortar: **1 tier**.
- Sniper: **4 tiers**.
- Anti-Armor / Air Defense: **4 tiers**.
- Heavy Gunner: **2 tiers**; primary MG/LSW ammunition is 100 rounds or more, while pistol/SMG ammunition follows its normal capacities.
- Medic: **4 tiers** (0 / 100 / 275 / 550 specialization XP).
- Helicopter Pilot: **1 tier**.

**Full progression details:** [Rank, XP, Operator Roles & Ranked Arsenal](PROGRESSION.md)

## Career continuity

Military rank, career points, role-specific specialization XP and ranked equipment unlocks persist independently of the active deployment. AO progress, vehicles, aircraft, logistics, objectives and battlefield state are deployment-specific.

**New battlefield. Same operator. Same career.**
